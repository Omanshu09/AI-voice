# Demo recording script (about 2 minutes)

Prep: open the Vercel URL in Chrome a minute early (wakes Render). Allow the mic when asked. Switch on **Hands-free**. Quiet room, headphones recommended.

| Time | Do / say | Show |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | "This is Asha, a voice-first Virtual KAM for Snapdeal sellers. The data is simulated." Click **Start conversation**. | Greeting spoken, Simulated badge |
| 0:15 | Say: "My sales have dropped a lot this month." | Asha explains 32.5% drop, +6 percentage points, 5 to 18 out of stock; insights table |
| 0:35 | Say: "Yes, some of my best sellers are out of stock." | Next question about restock; progress ticks Inventory |
| 0:50 | Interrupt mid-sentence by tapping the mic: "It will take about two weeks." | Interruption works |
| 1:05 | Say: "Mostly late dispatch." Then: "Visits are about the same." | Questions adapt, none repeated |
| 1:25 | Asha gives recommendations | Action plan with priority, steps, metric |
| 1:40 | Say: "Give me the summary." | Spoken summary and Call summary panel |
| 1:55 | Click **Download**. Mention: "Pricing is flagged as unsupported because the average price did not change." | Downloaded file, diagnosis panel |

Mention in closing: Gemini free tier with a validated local fallback; key stays on the backend.
